  • Your part. Connectors first. What it learns on one part carries to the next.

    Three-pin

  • Vision. A camera finds every part on the way in. No one lines it up.

  • Touch. Touch guides it in. It feels the part seat, so it knows when a part is really done.

  • Tester. Connected? Locked? Every part gets checked on the spot. The light says pass or fail.

  • Brain. Pass or fail, every try teaches it. No engineer rewriting code.

  • Reset. Good parts out. Bad parts aside. Next parts in. No one has to stand over it.
