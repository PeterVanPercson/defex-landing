CSV format and data handling

Columns: timestamp, product, decision, defect_class, confidence. Timestamps need a timezone. One row per inspection; PASS or FAIL; confidence from 0 to 1 or blank. Maximum 5,000 rows / 256 KB. Use non-identifying product codes.

The server calculates aggregates. Report generation sends counts, product and defect labels, hourly groups, and data-quality warnings to Nebius. It sends no images or raw inspection rows. Uploaded files and custom reports are not saved by this app. Nebius's own data policies apply.
