This page lists every cookie on defexrobotics.com and how to say no. Questions go to privacy@defexrobotics.com.

Ours.

We set two small cookies. The site needs both.

  • csrftoken stops other websites from sending our forms in your name. It is set when you open a page with a form, and kept for a year.
  • messages tells you whether your message or application was sent. It is set only after you send a form, and deleted when you close your browser.

Cal.com’s.

The booking calendar on our home page and on Why us comes from Cal.com. When it loads, Cal.com may set its own cookies, under Cal.com’s privacy policy. If you decline cookies, we do not load it in our pages, and the calendar opens on cal.com when you ask for it.

What we do not use.

No advertising cookies, and nothing that tracks you across other websites.

Your choice.

A small notice asks once. Your answer is kept in your browser, not in a cookie, and you can change it here.

You can also delete cookies in your browser’s settings. The form cookie comes back the next time you open a form.

Changes.

If this list changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
