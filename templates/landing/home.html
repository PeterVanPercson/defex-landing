Robots are cheap. Setting them up is not.

  1. Our robot teaches itself.

    Show it the job. It practices, tests its own work and gets better every time. No engineer programming every move.

  2. Bad parts never ship.

    Is it connected? Is it locked? Every part gets tested on the spot, with vision and touch.

  3. No one stands over it.

    Good parts out. Bad parts aside. Next parts in. It resets itself.

  4. A new part is not a new project.

    What it learns on one part carries to the next. Teach it what changed, not the whole job.
