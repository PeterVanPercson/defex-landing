A related connector arrives, and the alignment, the insertion and the fixture all get tuned again. A camera can tell you it looks right. It cannot tell you a contact never seated.

the test becomes the teacher

01

Attempt

The robot brings the connector to its mating half and tries the insertion, inside the bounds it is allowed to move in.

02

Test

Probes press onto the contacts and check the joint it just made. Continuity and mechanical engagement, not a photograph.

03

Reset

The fixture releases and reloads, so the next attempt runs without a person standing there.

Attempts are recorded and used to train candidate skills. A skill is evaluated on its own before it runs in production, so practice and production stay separate.
