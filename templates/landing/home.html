each new variant means more setup

01New variant
A related part arrives. Alignment, insertion and fixturing get tuned again from the start.
02Setup effort
Engineering hours go into making parts seat and join reliably, on every one of them.
03Unchecked joints
Appearance does not show a contact that never seated. That one leaves the line.

the test becomes the teacher

The robot attempts an assembly. A physical test scores what it just made. The recorded attempts train the next candidate skill, and a reset prepares another try. Practice runs in setup mode. Production runs a skill that was evaluated and approved on its own.

01Attempt
The robot tries the alignment and the insertion, inside bounds it is allowed to move in.
02Test
Continuity and mechanical engagement score the joint. Force and motion are recorded.
03Reset
The fixture returns the part to the start, so the next attempt runs without a person.

the first workstation is connector assembly

One robot. One fixtured connector family. Built for electronics and electromechanical assemblers who carry the setup cost every time a related variant arrives.

01Learns
Local alignment and insertion, on one qualified connector family.
02Checks
Electrical connection and mechanical engagement, on every attempt.
03Resets
The setup returns itself, ready for another practice attempt.
