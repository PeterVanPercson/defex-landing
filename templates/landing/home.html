We started off helping factory lines detect defects with vision.

That is how we learned the camera is not enough.

Robots are cheap. Setting them up is not.

  1. Practice needs a result.

    Our goal is a cell that uses assembly test results to improve its motion. The software prototype demonstrates a contact search and a reusable alignment correction.

  2. A connection needs two checks.

    Electrical continuity and mechanical retention answer different questions. Our prototype models both checks, including failures that a seated part alone cannot reveal.

  3. Make another attempt.

    The simulated tool retracts after each attempt. Physical part loading, recovery and unattended operation are still work ahead.

  4. Measure the next setup.

    We aim to reduce the engineering needed for related connector variants. Demonstrating that on customer parts is a future validation step.
