We started off helping factory lines detect defects with vision.

That is how we learned the camera is not enough.

Why us

  1. Robots you can walk away from.

    Ours reset and teach themselves, so a failed attempt comes back as a lesson. Not yours to fix.

  2. Our robots feel what’s wrong.

    A camera can tell you a part looks right. Ours physically test what they just built, so a defect is caught right away, with vision and touch.

  3. A new way, not a new project.

    Robots are harder to fixture than you think. Ours practice by themselves.
