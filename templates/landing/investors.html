Round open · updated {{ updated|date:"F j, Y" }}

Much of electronics assembly is still done by hand, because programming a robot for each new part variant costs more than the robot saves. We are building a cell that assembles a connector, tests the joint it just made, resets and tries again. Every attempt is scored by a physical test, so a new variant takes practice instead of weeks of hand programming.
