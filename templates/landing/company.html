We are building around one loop: attempt an assembly, physically test the connection, then reset for the next try. The goal is less setup work for the next supported part variant.

Our visual-inspection prototype is built. The assembly cell is in development. The next milestone is an unattended attempt, test and reset loop, followed by learning measured against a scripted baseline on fresh parts.

Watch the inspection prototype. Read the engineering approach.

The connection is the test.

The first application is connector assembly. Vision and force guide the insertion; electrical continuity and a mechanical engagement check evaluate the connection.

Practice and production are separate. Candidate skills are trained and evaluated on real parts. Production is designed to run only a qualified, approved skill.

We measure progress in setup time per variant, accepted output and operator interventions. These are the evaluation criteria, not published performance results.
