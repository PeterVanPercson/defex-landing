MODEL NOTES / V1

Inside the simulation.

A cartesian tool inserts an illustrative connector into a socket. MuJoCo computes the motion and contact forces in your browser. The 3D view follows those positions.

The search
A deterministic controller retracts after blocked contact and tries a ring of XY offsets. A successful, tested offset can be reused. It does not read the socket’s offset. This is calibration from feedback, not reinforcement learning.
The checks
Connection is a seating-depth check with an injectable open-circuit fault. Retention uses an ideal latch constraint and a simulated upward pull. There are no conductive materials or deforming latch mechanics in this model.
The limits
Geometry, friction and forces are illustrative. Only the plug housing and socket participate in contact. Tooling details are visual. The model omits vision, flexible wires, wear, damage, manufacturing tolerances and real robot dynamics. Reset releases the ideal latch and retracts the same plug; it does not model a fresh-part feeder.
The next proof
Build the physical insertion, test and reset loop, then compare setup effort and accepted output on customer parts. This page is not evidence of factory performance, learned skill transfer or manufacturing readiness.
